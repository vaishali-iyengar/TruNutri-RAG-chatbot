## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an authoritative, scientifically grounded nutrition and food safety companion. The aesthetic strikes a deliberate harmony between high-end culinary editorial design (reminiscent of curated food quarterlies) and modern conversational wellness technology. 

- **Personality:** Scholarly yet comforting, culinary, grounded, and unimpeachably reliable.
- **Audience:** Health-conscious individuals, culinary enthusiasts, and dietary navigators seeking rigorous, peer-reviewed clarity on ingredients, prep, and nutritional science without clinical coldness.
- **Emotional Response:** Comfort, reassurance, intellectual satisfaction, and deliberate calm.
- **Design Style:** Warm Editorial Minimalism blended with Soft Tactile Wellness. Surfaces evoke organic, linen-flecked paper stocks paired with deep vegetative forest tones, ochre archival accents, and pristine typographic hygiene.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-tech algorithmic guidance in agricultural and organic warmth. Deep botanicals, grain-rich papers, and sunlit accents signal trustworthiness and life sciences pedigree.

- **Primary (`#1C4B3D`):** Deep Forest Green. Anchors primary calls-to-action, user conversational bubbles, high-level headers, and active state triggers. Represents nature, precision, and vital balance.
  - *Dark Accent / Hover (`#13352B`):* Deep Evergreen for interactive hover states, micro-focus borders, and deep footer contrasts.
- **Secondary (`#DAA520`):** Warm Ochre / Goldenrod. Reserved strictly for citation identifiers, verification checkmarks, research tags, and key macronutrient badges. Evokes archival ink, warm sun, and clinical distinction.
  - *Subtle Ochre Surface (`#FDF8EE`):* Soft tinted container background for cited claims and source trays.
- **Tertiary (`#E07A5F`):** Muted Terracotta. Functional for temperature warnings, critical allergen flags, cross-contamination alerts, and urgent safety boundaries.
  - *Muted Terracotta Light (`#FDF1EE`):* Subtle alert backing for critical safety footnotes.
- **Neutral / Canvas:**
  - *Canvas Background (`#F7F2E9`):* Soft cream off-white, reducing eye fatigue during long reading sessions.
  - *Surface Cards (`#FAF7F2` & `#FFFFFF`):* Warm ivory and pure white elevated layered containers.
  - *Border / Separators (`#E6DFD3` or `rgba(28, 75, 61, 0.08)`):* Gentle sand-taupe hairline dividers.
- **Text Tones:**
  - *Text Primary (`#1A1A1A`):* Deep warm charcoal, avoiding cold digital blacks to preserve editorial integrity.
  - *Text Secondary (`#665F55`):* Grounded umber-gray for timestamps, metadata, and citation subtitles.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Playfair Display**—an expressive, transitional serif rich with historical authority and editorial weight—with **Plus Jakarta Sans**, a warm, exceptionally legible geometric sans-serif that balances modern clinical clarity with approachable human curves.

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys the intellectual weight of academic gastronomy and research institutions. Reserved for narrative milestones, conversational section breaks, dish names, and food safety rulings.
- **Body & Citations (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Handles continuous text, conversational bubbles, chemical compounds, and macronutrient breakdowns. Its open apertures maintain pristine readability even at small micro-label and source attribution sizes.
- **Rules of Balance:** Never use the serif display for interactive UI tokens, data inputs, or inline citations. Citations are strictly uppercase or tabular-aligned sans-serif to convey empirical precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial column system that mimics culinary journal layouts, prioritizing comfortable reading widths for dense scientific nutrition text.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, fluid-stretching with a minimum screen margin of `1rem`. Conversational feed takes full width.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Side panels or context trays can occupy 3 columns while chat feeds span 5 columns.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at an optimal reading max-width of `1280px` (`800px` for the primary conversational reading thread to avoid scan-line fatigue). Citations and food log summaries dock neatly in an adjacent 4-column reference drawer.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Built on an absolute 8px/4px base rhythm. Large horizontal cushions (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) surround bot responses to create a tranquil, unhurried atmosphere that differentiates this system from chaotic transactional chatbots.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, ambient, chromatic illumination instead of cold gray drop shadows. Depth mimics light passing through thick cotton cardstock sitting upon a wooden table.

- **Baseline Ambient Drop Shadows:** Shadows are color-cast using primary forest green rather than synthetic black: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 75, 61, 0.06)`. This imparts a radiant, organic warmth to lifted surfaces.
- **Tonal Layers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Base Canvas):* `#F7F2E9` (Soft Cream).
  - *Tier 1 (Surface Cards & AI Bubbles):* `#FAF7F2` or `#FFFFFF`, offset with subtle hairline borders (`1px solid #E6DFD3`).
  - *Tier 2 (Popovers, Source Previews & Drawers):* Elevated via `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(28, 75, 61, 0.10)`.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Outlines maintain a tactile, physical presence using warm sand/taupe borders (`#E6DFD3` or `rgba(28, 75, 61, 0.08)`) rather than stark vector cuts.

## Shapes

The design uses a generous curved geometry to evoke organic shapes and soften conversational friction.

- **Corners:** High standard radius (`roundedness: 2`, where base components use `0.5rem`, cards use `1rem` to `1.25rem` / `16px-20px`, and specialized prompt components deploy full pill profiles).
- **Conversational Bubbles:** Assistant message cards maintain generous 18px corners with an intentional soft flat edge on the reception side, feeling anchored and intentional.
- **Pill Primitives:** Buttons, interactive tags, search bars, and citation anchors leverage true pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`) to maintain fluid touch affordance and dynamic warmth.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep forest green (`#1C4B3D`) fill with crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), full pill shape (`9999px` radius), padding `12px 24px`. On hover, transitions to evergreen (`#13352B`) with a soft glow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(28, 75, 61, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Soft sand background (`#FAF7F2`), forest green text, framed by a `1px` border of `rgba(28, 75, 61, 0.2)`. On hover, fills with `#EAE3D5`.

### Chips & Citations
- **Citation Badges:** Compact pill chips (`border-radius: 9999px`, height `24px`, padding `0 10px`). Rendered with a warm ochre background (`#FDF8EE`), ochre text (`#996E08`), and a delicate border (`1px solid #E5B232`). Micro-copy uses uppercase sans-serif with bold bracket numbering: `[1] USDA FoodData`. On hover/tap, smoothly pops a source preview card.
- **Dietary Filter Chips:** Bordered pills with `#665F55` text and `#FAF7F2` background, switching to `#1C4B3D` fill with white text when selected.

### Input Fields & Chat Bar
- **Chat Input Bar:** Distinctive floating pill container (`border-radius: 9999px` or `24px`), ivory white base (`#FFFFFF`), wrapped in a subtle ambient shadow and a `1.5px` border of `#E6DFD3`. Focused state highlights border to forest green (`#1C4B3D`).
- **Send Trigger:** Circular internal button (`38px` diameter) in solid forest green with an ivory icon, resting nested within the right margin of the pill input.

### Conversational Cards & Assistant Responses
- **Assistant Card:** Generous padding (`24px`), ivory background (`#FAF7F2`), subtle ambient shadow (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 75, 61, 0.06)`), and rounded corners (`18px`).
- **User Bubble:** Distinctive deep forest green (`#1C4B3D`) pill/rounded-bubble, pure ivory text (`#FAF7F2`), positioned with clean right-aligned margin.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular radios and rounded square checkboxes (`radius: 6px`). Checked state fills with `#1C4B3D` with an ivory check/dot icon; idle state is framed with a `1.5px` border of `#665F55` on warm cream.

### Food Safety Alert Callout
- Special safety and recall card styled with a soft terracotta tint (`#FDF1EE`), deep terracotta left boundary (`3px solid #E07A5F`), and clear bold instructional copy for allergen and internal cooking temperature warnings.
